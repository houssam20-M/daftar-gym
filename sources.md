# Sources

The numbers the app uses for calories, protein and training, and where they come from.

## Calories

| What | Value in the app | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Resting burn (BMR) | Mifflin-St Jeor: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age, +5 for men, −161 for women | [Mifflin-St Jeor calculator](https://www.inchcalculator.com/mifflin-st-jeor-calculator/) |
| Activity factor | 1.375 for 3 days a week, up to 1.6 for 6-7 days; +0.1 for two sessions a day | Same source: 1.375 light (1-3 days), 1.55 moderate (3-5 days), 1.725 hard (6-7 days) |
| Losing weight | Deficit aimed at about 0.75% of body weight a week, never more than 1 kg a week | [Weight loss rate and lean mass (PMC review)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12323239): 0.5-1% a week keeps more muscle; 0.5 kg a week keeps more than 1 kg a week |
| Gaining weight | Surplus of 10% of the burn, between 150 and 350 kcal a day | [Lean bulk rate of gain (MacroFactor)](https://macrofactorapp.com/bulking-calculator/) and [rate of gain and lean mass (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8167794): slower gain is more muscle and less fat |

## Protein

| Goal | Value in the app | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Lose | 2.0 g per kg of goal weight | [ISSN position stand: protein and exercise, 2017](https://www.pubmed.gov/28642676/): 1.4-2.0 g/kg a day for people who train, more during a calorie deficit |
| Keep | 1.6 g per kg | Same |
| Gain | 1.8 g per kg | Same |

## Training

| What | Value in the app | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Volume for muscle | Each muscle trained 2-3 times a week, gaining goal uses 4 sets on the big lifts | [ACSM 2026 resistance training position stand](https://acsm.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Resistance-Training-Position-Stand-infographic.pdf): about 10 or more sets per muscle a week, every muscle at least 2 days a week |
| Losing weight with training | Weights kept in the plan, plus walking | [Weight loss rate and lean mass (PMC review)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12323239) |

## Notes

- These sources were found on 2026-11-17. Some are summaries of studies, not the studies themselves.
- All numbers are estimates. The weekly weigh-in is what decides: the app suggests 200 kcal more or less after two weeks if the weight moves too slowly or too fast.
